Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Summary

Money shows your bank balance and spending using your bank's alert emails and texts. Everything Money stores is kept in your own Google account. The developer of Money cannot see your data.

What Money accesses

  • Gmail (read-only): Money searches your email for bank transaction alerts (for example "debited", "credited", "UPI") and reads only those to find the amount, account, shop and balance. It never sends, changes or deletes email.
  • Google Sheets and Apps Script: during setup, Money creates a Google Sheet called "Money" and a copy of its program in your Google Drive. Your transactions are saved in that Sheet.
  • Text messages (Android app only, if you allow it): the app reads texts that look like bank alerts (from business senders, mentioning money) and sends them to your own Sheet. Texts from people's phone numbers are never read or sent.
  • Your email address: used only to show which account you are setting up.

Where your data goes

Your data stays in your Google account. It is not sent to the developer, not sold, not shared, and not used for advertising. The only other service involved is Google itself, which hosts your Sheet and runs your copy of Money.

If you use the optional "Ask" feature on iPhone, your question and a summary of your numbers are processed by Apple Intelligence on your device (or Apple's Private Cloud Compute).

Removing your data

Delete the "Money" Sheet from your Google Drive, and remove Money's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. On Android, uninstall the app.

Google API Services

Money's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
