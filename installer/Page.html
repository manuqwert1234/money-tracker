Money

Track your bank balance and spending from your bank's emails and texts. Free. Everything is created in your own Google account. Nobody else, including the person who shared this, can see your money.

Setting up Money in your Google account

About 20 seconds. Keep this page open.

One switch first

Google needs one switch turned on, once. Tap the button, turn on Google Apps Script API, then come back to this page.

Open the switch
Last step: allow Money to read your bank emails

If Google says "Google hasn't verified this app", tap Advanced, then Go to Money, then Allow. It is your own private copy, so that warning is expected.
